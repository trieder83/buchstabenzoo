# Brief — `env_goldfish`

Spec: ART-ENVIRONMENT, GAME-LEVEL-3. Status: **brief — images not generated yet**. Layout: `layout.md` in this
folder; level data `assets/levels/level-3.toml`.

## Purpose

Start and end of the goldfish mission: the board says the fish needs a bowl of water; at the end the child puts the bowl with the fish on the stone step (the gate) and the fish jumps into its pond. Empty at level start.

## Must be visible

- Low wooden fence 11 × 10 m around a round pond with a low stone rim (`goldfish_pond`, 8 × 8 m), water plants, two flat stones in the water.
- The "gate": a flat stone step in the fence on the west side where the bowl is put down; sign with the goldfish silhouette, info board.

## Must not appear

- No waterfall, no water wheel, no willow (goldfish hiding places).
- No fish (empty at level start).

## Props used

Modular (ART-ENVIRONMENT list): `fence_wood`, `enclosure_sign`, `info_board`, `path_tile`, `grass_tuft`, `reed`, `rock`, `fish_bowl`.
Unique models: `pond_stone_rim`.
