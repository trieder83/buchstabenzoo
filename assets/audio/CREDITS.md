# Audio credits and licences (ART-SOUND)

Every cue is listed in `assets/manifest.toml` (`kind = "audio"`). Licence per cue:

| Licence | Cues | Attribution needed |
|---|---|---|
| `own` | all synthesised cues (doors, pickups, UI): generated by `tools/sound/*.py` (numpy/scipy, seeded) | no |
| `generated` | `step_water_*` (Runway `eleven_text_to_sound_v2`; Runway terms: outputs commercially usable, Q-250) | no |
| `CC0` / `public-domain` | animal cues and `step_path/grass/sand/wood_*` from real recordings (tables below) | no |
| `CC-BY` | `animal_koala_*`, `animal_kiwi_*`, `animal_raccoon_*` | yes, lines below |

No CC-NC, ND or unclear licences are used.

## CC-BY credits (shown on the in-game credits page)

- `animal_koala_call`, `animal_koala_happy`, `animal_koala_refuse`, `animal_koala_baby`: koala bellow, Charlton, Ellis, McKinnon, Brumm, Nilsson, Fitch (2011), PLoS ONE, Movie S1, CC BY 2.5, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Perception-of-Male-Caller-Identity-in-Koalas-(Phascolarctos-cinereus)-Acoustic-Analysis-and-pone.0020329.s001.ogv . Cut, pitched and filtered.
- `animal_kiwi_call`, `animal_kiwi_happy`, `animal_kiwi_refuse`: male brown kiwi call, Department of Conservation (New Zealand), CC BY 4.0, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Male-ni-brown-kiwi.wav . Cut, pitched and filtered.
- `animal_raccoon_call`, `animal_raccoon_happy`, `animal_raccoon_refuse`: raccoon chatter, jnargus (video), cut by AntumDeluge, CC BY 3.0 or later, https://opengameart.org/content/raccoon-chatter . Cut, pitched and filtered.
