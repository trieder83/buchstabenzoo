The animals have escaped from the zoo, and every enclosure is empty! Can you read the clues and bring them all home?

Letter Zoo is a 3D reading adventure for children from kindergarten to grade 3. Reading is the key to the game: you cannot win by guessing.

HOW IT WORKS
- Read the riddle on an enclosure's info board. It tells you where the animal is hiding.
- Read the labels on the food boxes and pick the right food for that animal.
- Find the animal, show it the food and lead it safely back to its enclosure.
- Watch it eat and cheer. Then on to the next empty enclosure!

READING AT YOUR LEVEL
Choose kindergarten, grade 1, grade 2 or grade 3. The riddles and food labels match the level, with simple pictures to help beginners. Optional maths tasks for grades 1 to 5 can be switched on too.

EXPLORE THE ZOO
- Walk through a friendly comic-style zoo by day, and discover a night zoo with lanterns.
- Look for many different animals, from zebras and koalas to elephants and owls.
- Ride the golf cart, look through the telescope and meet the zoo visitors.
- Animals can even have babies when they get their favourite treat at home.

MADE FOR CHILDREN
- No ads and no in-app purchases
- No accounts and no data collection
- Small posters in the zoo show other apps by the same developer; they open only behind a parent check
- No pressure: there is no timer, so take all the time you need
- Works fully offline, with large, easy buttons for touch screens
- Available in English and German

Letter Zoo was made to make reading practice feel like an adventure. Come on, the animals are waiting!
